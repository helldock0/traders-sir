Then repeat the 0.04 gap test in the check period at the chosen (k, hold); failing it is a kill. 4) Pooled t-statistics in stage B, check and walk-forward are computed on date-clustered R: average the R of all markets that traded the same signal date into one observation, then compute t over dates. Report the unclustered figure alongside. 5) Exit timing: put the exit flag on the last bar before E(D + hold_days), not on E(D + hold_days). The trade then closes at the open of the bar that ends at 20:00 UTC on that date, and a new shock signal at E(D + hold_days) can register. With the rule as written, the executor sees the position still open when the next day's signal arrives and silently drops it. Shock days cluster, so that loses exactly the follow-on shocks. 6) Keep kill rules (1), (3) and (4) as written. Update expected numbers: per-market design t about 0.6-1.2 at 0.08 R, so stage A on EURUSD alone has a real chance of a false negative. The Director should know this before reading a stage A kill.

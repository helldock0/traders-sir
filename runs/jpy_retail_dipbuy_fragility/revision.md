# Pre-registration amendment (cycle 9, before any run): jpy_retail_dipbuy_fragility

Applied the Critic's 'revise' verdict in full (runs/cycles/cycle_0009/critic_review.json, written before any backtest):

1. R1 Planned-hold de-overlap replaces the leftover guard: qualifying rows of the active band are walked in time order and kept only if the signal bar is at or after the exit target of the last kept row; dropped rows set no entry and no markers; a trade stopped early does not reopen the window. Five-day declines qualify on runs of consecutive days, so the leftover guard would have cut most 2- and 4-day holds to about one day and skipped every other qualifying day: the hold the hypothesis is about would not have been tested.
2. R2 Friday rows trade at the Monday Tokyo open: T* = the first 00:00 UTC at or after ts_avail that falls on Monday to Friday (ts_avail itself for Monday-Thursday rows, the following Monday 00:00 UTC for Friday rows); the signal bar is the bar whose close time is T* (the 23:00-00:00 UTC bar, which on Sunday night belongs to Monday's trading day), otherwise the first bar closing within max_delay_min of T* on T*'s UTC date; D = daily.day_idx[sig] - 1 and daily.date[D] must equal the row's date. This restores the 20 percent of rows (Fridays) that the original rule silently skipped.

Director's additional change, folding in the parallel session's Critic on the same mechanism (runs/cycles/cycle_0009/critic_review_fork_slugs_38_39.json, slug jpy_retail_dip_buying_cascade, superseded): the dip-buying signature (a negative yearly correlation between the daily return and the open-interest change) holds in every year 2010-2023 only on AUDJPY (-0.40 to -0.79) and NZDJPY (-0.24 to -0.78); USDJPY flips positive from 2017, GBPJPY is erratic, EURJPY shows none; the weekly long share in 2022-23 was 0.51 AUDJPY, 0.50 NZDJPY, 0.32 GBPJPY, 0.35 EURJPY, 0.29 USDJPY. The premise of the mechanism (a structurally long, contrarian retail book) therefore holds only on AUDJPY and NZDJPY, and the gated markets are narrowed to those two: spec.markets = [AUDJPY, NZDJPY]. The pooled-check hard gate rests on AUDJPY and NZDJPY (about 90 heavy10 check trades at k 1.0, hold 2). A USDJPY, GBPJPY or EURJPY heavy arm as strong as AUDJPY's is evidence that the signal is the decline, not the book.

9. Director amendment after the build, before any run (no outcome seen): the fixed parameter exit_utc moves from '20:30' to '20:00' UTC. Reason, found by the Builder from bar timestamps only: on H1 bars the last Friday bar in US daylight time opens at 20:00 UTC, so 'the first bar at or after 20:30' on a Friday is the Sunday evening open; 24 of 137 default-cell entries would have carried the weekend gap and extra rollover nights, and the Monday-to-Thursday exits would have sat at the 21:00 UTC bar, the 17:00 New York rollover hour in summer when spreads widen. With 20:00 the exit is at the open of the 20:00 UTC bar on every weekday (16:00 New York in summer, 15:00 in winter). Changed in specs/auto/jpy_retail_dipbuy_fragility.json, runs/jpy_retail_dipbuy_fragility/hypothesis.json and the module default. Holiday gaps (14 cases, 25 December and 1 January, where the 20:00 bar is missing) exit at the first bar after the gap, causally.

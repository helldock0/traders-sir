# Pre-registration amendment (cycle 9, before any run): fx_surprise_against_crowd

Applied the Critic's 'revise' verdict in full (runs/cycles/cycle_0009/critic_review.json, written before any backtest):

1. R1 Late releases: a release is skipped entirely unless xi > sig + 1, where xi is the first bar at or after the exit target. This removes the NZD releases at 21:45 and 22:45 UTC from the d0 hold (they would otherwise fill after the 20:30 UTC target and become one-bar trades on the New York rollover spread).
2. R2 Planned-hold de-overlap replaces the leftover guard: releases of the active arm are walked in ts_avail order and kept only if the signal bar is at or after the exit target of the last kept release; dropped releases set no entry and no markers; a trade stopped early does not reopen the window. The leftover guard would have closed the open trade at the next release's fill bar while the executor, not flat at the signal close, ignored the new entry, so 10 to 30 percent of d1 releases would have been cut short with nothing in their place and the arms would have differed for a mechanical reason.
3. R3 PARAMS and GRID unchanged (24 cells in the module).
4. R4 Spec edit done: specs/auto/fx_surprise_against_crowd.json now has stage_a = {market: EURUSD, grid: all 24 cells} and grid = {arm: [against], crowd_q: [0.8, 0.9], z_min: [1.0, 1.5], hold: [d0, d1]} (8 cells), so stages B, C, the walk-forward and any holdout run the hypothesis arm only and the automatic gates are never computed on a control cell.
5. R5 Restated expectations: the leveraged-fund series in cot.parquet starts 2010-07-20, so the first 52-week rank is usable 2011-07-15 and design trades run 2011-07 to 2017-12 (6.5 years); 2010 has none. EURUSD design releases against / with / neutral: 101 / 113 / 172 at q 0.8, z 1.0; 69 / 77 / 240 at q 0.9, z 1.0; 49 / 51 / 94 at q 0.8, z 1.5; 32 / 33 / 129 at q 0.9, z 1.5 (the brief's neutral counts wrongly included releases with no rank). After R1-R2 the q 0.8, z 1.0 arms hold about 98 / 106 / 162 trades (d0) and 86 / 92 / 127 (d1). The q 0.9, z 1.5 against cell (32 releases) can never reach the 40-trade minimum and cannot be chosen.
6. R6 Kill rule (1) reads 'among the 8 against cells'. Kill rule (4) reads: at least 4 of the 7 years 2011-2017 positive at the chosen cell (2011 = July to December).
7. R7 Mechanism gate before any promotion: if the against arm passes B/C, the Director runs the matching with and neutral cells (same crowd_q, z_min, hold) on GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDJPY and USDCAD with end = 2024-01-01 (design and check only, counted as trials). Rule (2) is judged on the pooled six-market trades: against minus with >= +0.08 R and against minus neutral >= +0.05 R in design, both > 0 in check. The EURUSD stage A asymmetry alone (standard error about 0.1 R) is not enough.
8. R8 Breadth rule (3): a merged release takes the news side of the currency with the larger sum of |surprise_z| among its rows; USDCAD's 136 merged USD+CAD releases are also reported as 'mixed'.
9. R9 Reporting: each arm's avg R split by whether the trade agrees with the sign of the pair's 63-trading-day close-to-close change at the signal bar (crowding follows trend, so the against arm is mostly counter-trend); NZDUSD NZD-news fills reported separately with fill spread in pips and as a share of the stop.
10. R10 A neutral-arm carry-forward is allowed only as a new hypothesis judged on the five markets where the neutral arm was never run, plus the holdout; its EURUSD design and check results are spent.
11. R11 The 'timing filter on the parked USDJPY legs' path is struck (not pre-registered). Speed is judged by the monthly Sharpe of the pooled six-market R per month after costs, which accounts for the same-minute US-news correlation; below 0.7 the result is recorded as an edge, not traded.

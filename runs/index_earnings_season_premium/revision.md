The fill is at the open of the 20:00 UTC bar, which is 16:00 New York during daylight saving and 15:00 in winter. State the real fill times in the rules: entry at the open of the 15:00 UTC bar (H1 bars are stamped at the open), exit at the open of the 20:00 UTC bar.
2) Decide the entry from Monday information only: ei found, the bar no more than 90 minutes after Monday 14:30, ATR20 finite and > 0, date >= start, and the arm's week selection. Delete every skip that depends on the Friday bar. Exit plumbing: (a) normal exit: xi = first bar at or after Friday exit_utc; if xi < m.n, xi > ei and m.ts[xi] is no more than max_exit_delay_min late, set exit_long[xi - 1] = True. (b) Fallback: set exit_long[j] = True on every bar j of that week with ts in [Friday 20:00 UTC, following Monday 12:00 UTC). A week whose Friday session is missing or cut short then exits at the first bar after the gap instead of running into the next trade week. Report the number of fallback (non-Friday) exits in each arm.
3) Set start to '2013-01-01' (2012 index bars have no spread in the data; 2011 has six days). Expected design (2013-2017) counts: about 70-90 season weeks per cell and about 140-170 control weeks.
4) Replace kill rule 2 with a quarter-paired test. For the chosen cell and its matching ctrl cell (same start_off, n_weeks and sl_atr), compute D_q = mean R of the season weeks in calendar quarter q minus mean R of the non-season weeks in the same quarter, using quarters with at least one week in each arm. Require mean D > 0 with t(D over quarters) >= 1.0 in design, and mean D > 0 in check. This threshold matches the pre-registered +0.12 R against about 0 R. Rule 7 (promotion) requires revised rule 2. The holdout keeps the pre-registered 'chosen minus matching ctrl > 0'.
5) FOMC attribution: report D in design and in check after dropping, from both arms, every week that contains a scheduled FOMC decision. Take the dates from the 'Federal Funds Rate' rows in data/external/calendar_spoluan/forex_factory_calendar_YYYY.csv. If the design D excluding FOMC weeks is <= 0, scrap with the lesson that the season excess is the pre-FOMC drift already measured in prior_fomc_cycle.
6) Score replication on the difference, not on raw season R. Run the matching ctrl cell of the chosen season cell once each on USA500IDXUSD and JPNIDXJPY (counted trials, design and check only, horizon cut at the holdout start, for example scripts/run_backtest.py with --end 2024-01-01).
